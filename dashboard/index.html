    About Earthcraft

    Earthcraft recreates places in Minecraft at one block per metre. Maps and elevation data supply terrain; LiDAR adds building shape where scans exist.

    Street-photo color, windows, and entrances are still planned. The saved footprint does not verify current block fill.
